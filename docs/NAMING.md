# Naming — History & Candidates

**Current working name: CulinaryCoreOS (CCOS).** Not final — see
`docs/DECISIONS.md`. Claude has a standing reminder to periodically
revisit this before trademark filing / public launch.

## Why it needs revisiting
"CulinaryCore" started as a generic placeholder in the original SRS draft —
not derived from the source data, not connected to the actual originating
business (Chupacabras/Arriba, Bali), and fairly generic-sounding compared to
competitors. "OS" was appended to signal the platform's growth beyond a
recipe manager into a full hospitality operating system.

## Candidates brainstormed so far (2026-07-26)

**Structural / systems-oriented**
- Mise — from *mise en place*; short, industry-native, easy to trademark
- Batchly — evokes batch costing/production planning
- Yieldworks — nods to yield/waste management
- Larderstack — pantry/storage + tech platform
- Coverline — kitchen term for the line during service

**Abstract / brandable**
- Passform — "pass" (kitchen pass) + platform
- Kitchara — invented, food-adjacent, clean phonetics
- Provisio — from "provision"; procurement + foresight
- Ferment (e.g. "Ferment Platform") — growth/transformation metaphor

**Compound/enterprise-style**
- GastroCore, KitchenOS, Recipeworks, HospitalityStack, CulinaryCoreOS

None of these have had a full clearance check (domain/trademark/App Store)
yet — that was deferred in favor of unblocking development. Do this before
committing to anything publicly.
